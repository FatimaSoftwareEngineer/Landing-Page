Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Aura One before you buy.

How long does the battery last?

Up to 40 hours of playback on one charge. A 10-minute fast charge gives you about 5 hours.

Do the headphones work with iPhone and Android?

Yes. They connect over Bluetooth 5.3, so they work with any phone, tablet or laptop.

How do I pair them with my phone?

Hold the power button for three seconds until the light blinks, then choose Aura One in your Bluetooth settings.

Can I use them with a cable?

Yes. Plug in the USB-C cable and you can keep listening while they charge.

What does the warranty cover?

Manufacturing faults and battery problems. The warranty is 1, 2 or 3 years, depending on your plan.

Can I return them if I don't like them?

Yes. You have 30 days to try them and send them back for a full refund.
